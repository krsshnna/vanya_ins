Admissions 2027–28

Clear eligibility criteria, step-by-step admission guidance, scholarship pathways, and our interactive demonstration application form.

How to Apply

Four-Step Admission Process

A transparent, merit-conscious process for both undergraduate and postgraduate applicants.

01

Submit Application

Complete the online admission form below with academic records and preferred degree programme.

02

Document Verification

Applicants present original mark sheets, transfer certificates, and identity proof at the campus desk.

03

Merit Review & Counselling

Faculty advisors review academic eligibility and guide students on subject combinations and scholarships.

04

Fee & Enrolment

Confirm admission by completing annual tuition payment and receiving your student orientation schedule.

Eligibility

Eligibility Requirements

  • Undergraduate (B.Com, B.Com Hons., BBA, BCA, BA, B.Sc): Pass in 10+2 (Senior Secondary) or equivalent from a recognised board. Specific streams apply for B.Com Hons. (Commerce/Maths) and B.Sc (Science).
  • Postgraduate (M.Com, MA, M.Sc): Completed a 3-year Bachelor’s degree in the relevant discipline from a recognised university.
  • Provisional Applications: Candidates awaiting final board or university results may submit an application provisionally.
Checklist

Required Documents

  • Class 10 & Class 12 mark sheets and passing certificates (self-attested + originals for verification).
  • Graduation mark sheets and provisional/degree certificate (for M.Com, MA, and M.Sc applicants).
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) / Migration Certificate from previous institution.
  • Government-issued photo ID proof and 4 recent passport-size photographs.
  • Income or merit certificates (if applying for scholarship consideration).

Financial Aid

Scholarships & Fee Support

Illustrative institutional scholarship categories aimed at encouraging academic diligence and equitable access:

Merit Academic Waiver

Partial first-year tuition fee waivers for incoming applicants securing 85% or higher in qualifying board or university examinations.

Need-Based Student Assistance

Flexible instalment plans and tuition concessions for students from economically modest households upon submitting family income documentation.

Sports & Cultural Encouragement Grant

Fee concessions for students actively representing the institute in inter-collegiate athletics, debate, music, or community service.
