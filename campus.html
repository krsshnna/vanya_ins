Campus Ecosystem

Facilities & Student Engagement

Nine dedicated pillars supporting academic discovery, well-being, creativity, and career readiness.

Modern Classrooms

Bright, well-ventilated lecture halls featuring ergonomic seating, clear sightlines, and digital interactive boards for engaging instruction.

Computer Laboratories

High-speed networked computing labs equipped for software development, statistical software, Tally/accounting tools, and scientific computing.

Digital Library

A peaceful two-level reading sanctuary housing reference textbooks, academic periodicals, group study pods, and e-library terminals.

Sports Facilities

Indoor table tennis, badminton, and outdoor basketball courts alongside cricket practice nets encouraging balanced student fitness.

Cafeteria

Hygienic, sunlit dining spaces offering freshly cooked vegetarian meals, snacks, and refreshments at student-friendly prices.

Cultural Events

Annual cultural festivals, music and theatre evenings, Independence & Republic Day programmes, and inter-college literary meets.

Student Clubs

Student-driven Coding Society, Commerce & Finance Forum, Debate & Literary Circle, Photography Club, and NSS Community Wing.

Seminars & Workshops

Guest lectures by practitioners, research paper symposia, and weekend skill bootcamps bridging classroom theory with real-world practice.

Career Guidance

Dedicated career guidance cell conducting resume workshops, mock interviews, communication clinics, and postgraduate entrance counselling.
